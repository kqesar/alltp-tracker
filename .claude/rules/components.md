# UI, styles and accessibility

ALttPR's modules live under `apps/web/src/games/alttpr/`: a short path below (`logic/…`, `stores/gameStore.ts`, `map/…`, `autotrack/follow.ts`) is relative to it, unless it names one of the shared modules (`components/ui/` Modal, Tabs, ConfirmDialog, ToolbarMenu, ToolbarIcons, UpdatePrompt, Header; `autotrack/guide.ts`, `autotrack/status.ts`; `hooks/` useGridNavigation, useNow, useToolbarFit, useTooltip; `stores/` tooltipStore, updateStore; `utils/`; `styles/`).

## Components

- `tracker/` — `TrackerGrid` → `grid/GridItem` routes a cell: empty → spacer, `keysN` → `BigKeyCell`, the key cell of key dungeon N (`KEY_DUNGEONS` in `logic/settings.ts`: the grid's ten, then Hyrule Castle, Castle Tower and Ganon's Tower on the 8th row) (`KeyToggle` buttons for what its pool holds, `ownItems`: the big key and the small keys, none for Eastern Palace, whose pool has no small key, no big key for Hyrule Castle or Castle Tower; in runs whose maps and compasses leave their dungeons (MC, MCS and Full Shuffle: `wildItems` in `data/presets.ts`) only, the map and compass on top, the small keys' count then a badge on the key), `keylabelN` → `KeyLabel` (a castle dungeon's short name, HC and GT, as keysanity trackers label their key rows; decorative: Castle Tower has Agahnim's cell, `agahnim`, in its place, a cell that stays when the key cells go), the half magic a plain `Item` between the tunic and the boots (`magic`, in `TOGGLE_ITEMS`: its click toggles none and half, whatever the value, and a quarter magic read from the game is kept for the logic and drawn, named and stepped as the half), in a retro run no small key (generic keys are no dungeon's) and, on the 8th row, the shops' cells (`itemGridFor`), the key cells' rows showing only with the key cells (`visibleRows`; `layout--key-rows` sizes `--cell` for 8 rows), `tunic` → `TunicCell` (a `<fieldset>` "Mail and hearts": the tunic's `Item` and `overlays/HeartCounter.tsx`, Link's hearts on screen as a number in the bottom right corner over a heart filled by the pieces toward the next as the pause menu draws them (`heart0.png` to `heart3.png`: top left, bottom left, top right; the number sits on the bottom right quarter, which no count of pieces fills; drawn with `heart.png` by `apps/web/scripts/heart-icon.mjs`, our own sprites), named "Hearts: 13 and 2 quarters", a click a quarter more (`heartsAfter`), "?" over the full heart and dimmed while not set, slot 1), `triforce` → `PiecesCell` (a Triforce Hunt's last row, centred: the pieces found over the pieces asked for, "12/20", a button named "Triforce pieces: 12/20. Click to add a piece.", `griditem--dimmed` at none, `pieces-cell--enough` once the goal's are held; `triforce.png`, drawn by `apps/web/scripts/triforce-icon.mjs`, our own sprite; a click a piece, a right-click or "-" one off, stopping at 0 and at 50; the layout's `--grid-rows` counts the row, `layout--pieces-row`), `boss*` → `BossCell` (a `<fieldset>` named after the dungeon: the boss button plus `KnownBossPicker` (top left, `known-boss-overlay-N`, "Misery Mire boss: Blind": the boss the dungeon holds under boss shuffle, a lettered `?` while not known (`overlay--text`, unlike a medallion's coin), else the boss's own sprite, `bossN2.png` in the pack's order; only while `knownBossShown`; the dungeon's tooltip names it too, `tooltip-known-boss`, "Boss not known" until marked), `MedallionPicker`, `ChestCounter`, `RewardPicker` as sibling buttons from `overlays/BossOverlays.tsx`, in that reading order (slots 1 to 4); Ganon's Tower's, `boss12` at the end of the 8th row, which therefore always shows, holds Agahnim 2 (`agahnim2`) under its short name, GT (decorative, so its Agahnim is not taken for Castle Tower's), and its items left (`chest12`, up to 27: the `chestN.png` counters run to 27) and no reward), else → `Item`.
- Every grid control spreads `useGridFocusable({ row, col, slot })` (roving tab index, arrow-key position; slot 0 is the cell's main control, then reading order) and `useStepHandlers(step, useControlShortcuts(control).aria)` (click forward, right-click or "-" back, touch long-press ignored; `aria-keyshortcuts` lists the control's keyboard shortcuts and "-"). Never put a button inside another.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `hooks/useItemShortcuts.ts` is the one window listener; a bound key steps its control through `stepControl`, exactly as a click does. It shares `isAwayFromBoard` (`apps/web/src/utils/keyboard.ts`: typing in a field, a dialog open) with `games/useUndoShortcuts.ts` (one hook, given each game's store), skips IME composition and auto-repeat, and never takes a Ctrl/Cmd chord or a key that keeps its own job (`refusalOf` in `data/keymap.ts`). The focused cell's "-" gives way to a shortcut on the same physical key.
- `map/MapTracker.tsx` draws two square worlds (`map-world-light` / `-dark`), side by side, stacked or `auto` (stacks below 35rem via a container query). Coordinates are authored against the whole asset and placed in their world by `toWorldPosition` (`apps/web/src/utils`): the alltpr pack's markers are in that space (percent of the whole image). It draws the run's world's (`markersOf`): an Inverted run moves Link's House to the Dark World, puts the Bomb Merchant in its place and swaps Castle Tower's and Ganon's Tower's doors.
- `map/MapMarkers.tsx` — `MapLocation` (one location or a cave's several: a click checks them all, or none once all are) and `MapDungeon` (a decorative, `aria-hidden` square showing the counter by the pigeonhole rule, under the boss button, which toggles the boss's grid flag) are `<button>`s named by the marker's name plus their state, with a glyph per state besides the colour: ✓ available, ? possible, an eye (a CSS mask, since emoji fonts colour the character) for seen — out of logic but in sight, from the engine's visible channel —, a lightning bolt on purple (`--state-trick`, 3.5:1 with `--state-ink`; a mask too, but drawn in place on a boss's round badge, which keeps its disc) for trick — out of logic but opened by a trick the player knows (`trickSolutionOf`) —, a heart on orange (`--state-margin`, a mask the same way, drawn in place on a boss's badge) for margin — out of logic by basic placement's safety margin alone (`marginSolutionOf`) —, ✕ out of logic. The precedence is `locationStatus`'s: available > possible > margin > trick > seen > unavailable. The states' words are `STATE_LABELS` ("out of logic, doable with a trick", "out of logic, doable outside Basic's safety margin") and the tooltip's "Doable with a trick", "Doable, outside Basic's safety margin". They read their state through the hooks of `apps/web/src/games/alttpr/logic/solution.ts` (`useMarkerStatus`, `useBossStatus`), which return strings, so a click re-renders only the markers it changed. Markers keep a 24 px floor; where real locations sit closer than that, WCAG 2.5.8's map-pin exception applies and the e2e target-size check skips `.mapspan`.
- `map/MapPanel.tsx` — the map panel: ARIA tabs Map | Checks | Run, with Entrances after Checks while entrances are shuffled (`ui/Tabs.tsx`) over one tabpanel, `#map-view`, which shows the worlds and their legend, `ChecksPanel` or `RunPanel`, by the `mapView` preference; `VoiceControl`, `RunTimer` and `GoMode` sit beside the tabs (`map-panel__status`). Over the map and the checks, `LogicNote` says why every check is maybe while the run's logic is not modelled (`unmodelledReasons`: every cause by name, the glitched logic, the entrance shuffle, shuffled bosses behind Crossworld entrances (`bossesMayMove`)), and in a `crossedLogic` run that the logic follows the entrances noted. "Skip to map" and "Skip to checks" render their view with `flushSync`, then focus `#map-view`.
- `map/entrances/` — the entrance assistant, for a run with shuffled entrances, each module loaded on demand with the pack's entrance tables (`logic/entrances.ts`): `EntranceLayer` (a shuffled world's markers, each check inside a place the shuffle mixes standing above the first door noted to lead there or nowhere yet (`placeMarkers`; the Dam's, half outdoors, stays), then its shuffled doors and holes as round `mapspan` buttons; pointing at a noted one rings every entrance into the same place (`entrancePickerStore.linked`); an empty dashed ring while not known, filled by the kind of place once noted with a glyph for each, named "Kakariko Shop: leads to Elder House (East)", with a `text` tooltip), `EntrancePicker` (the `Modal` "Where does … lead?": a search, then the targets the shuffle may give it by kind as buttons (`targetsOf`), a hole and its cave's door noted together as one step (`notesFor`, `noteEntrances`); one noted elsewhere says where, and choosing it moves it; "Not known" forgets one) and `EntrancesPanel` (the Entrances tab: every entrance by world, its destination beside it, a search, how many are noted and that autotracking notes each door gone through outside Race Mode). The markers and the list open the picker through `entrancePickerStore`.
- `map/ChecksPanel.tsx` — every location of the map's markers as a list, by zone (`logic/checks.ts`: a dungeon's under its name, any other under its upstream region's). Status filters with their counts (available and possible shown at first; the Outside margin filter exists only while `marginShown` and the Trick filter only while `tricksShown`, both unticked like Seen), a zone `<select>` and a search narrow it, and a `role="status"` summary says how many rows show. A row is a checkbox calling `toggleChecks([id])`, its state in words beside the map's glyph (`check-swatch state-glyph`), for a check in the trick state the tricks that open it under its name (`check-trick-<id>`, a `TrickWays` chip ringed in `--state-trick`, inside the label so the checkbox's name ends ", doable with Fake flipper"; mounted for those rows alone, so only they follow the analysis, and nothing while it is pending), and for a state other than available or done a "Why?" disclosure with `MissingLines`, mounted only once opened (with the tricks' guides linked, and an **About tricks** button opening the `TricksDialog` for a check a trick opens), and only while the run's logic is applied (`solutionOf(…).modelled`: not while a crossed run's logic loads). A dungeon's heading (a sticky `checks-zone__head`: the `h3` and the button) has a "What's missing?" disclosure of the same kind with `MissingGroups` over every check of the dungeon, whichever the filters show ("Working it out…" until the analysis is in), offered while something in the dungeon is not available or done (a boss alive counts, whatever the counter). A dungeon counted out (`isCountedOut`: the counter at 0, no check but the boss's ticked) shows "no item left" beside its name (whenever the counter is 0) and its rows checked and disabled, but for its boss row (the pack's `bossLocation`), which follows the boss: unchecked, and as disabled as the rest, until the boss is marked beaten (the counter counts items, not the kill, and no box could change that). The panel subscribes to every state at once (`checkStates` through `useShallow`) and rows are memoized; a row a filter hides once ticked hands its focus to the next row. Zone headings are sticky and take the page's fixed background (`.page-background`). Beside the grid it scrolls inside the viewport's height; under it, it grows with the page.
- `map/RunPanel.tsx` — the Run tab: the run at a glance (`<dl>`: the timer's time, ticking only while it runs; the checks done, `checkStates`; the bosses beaten), the splits (a `<table>`: the bosses in the order they fell, from `splitsOf` in `logic/runLog.ts`, each with its time and the segment since the last, then the goal once the timer stopped there), the timeline (an `<ol>`, newest first: each find's `<time>`, or a dash said as "before the timer", its sprite as the board draws it, `findIcon`, and its name, `findName`; rows memoized; in Race Mode, or on Auto while autotracking is starting or set to connect and no reading has told the flag (`isRaceModeOrPending`), `findsOf(timeline, true)` keeps the items alone, under a note `run-finds-race`: v2.0 forbids tying an item to a location, and a check's time beside an item's would; the stamps stay and show once Race Mode is off) and "From the game", the ROM's own stats (`reading.progress.romStats`, in the end credits' order) outside races once a file is loaded, or why not. Beside the grid each column scrolls inside the viewport's height; under it, the panel grows with the page. It reads the timeline (`logic/timeline.ts`) with one selector.
- `map/GoMode.tsx` — the run's go mode (`goModeStatusOf`): ☆ not yet, ★? possible, ★♥ outside Basic's margin (`goModeMarginOf`: not yet, but every step open without basic placement's safety margin; `go-mode--margin`, its panel leading with `go-mode-margin` and its ways "In logic with"), ★ in logic, with its words; hidden for a goal without one (a Triforce Hunt's is its count: "7 pieces left", its panel a sentence with the pieces left), and a plain label "Go mode: goal unknown" while a Mystery run's goal is hidden. A visually hidden `role="status"` announces a change of state, never of count.

## Semantics and accessibility

- Every interactive element is a semantic `<button>` (or link) with an accessible name; never a clickable `div`, and never a button inside a button. A native button fires click on Enter and Space, so add no key handlers for them.
- Label in Name (WCAG 2.5.3, axe's `label-content-name-mismatch`): an `aria-label` starts with, or at least contains in order, the control's visible text, and axe reads the text of adjacent elements with nothing between them ("Autotracking" + "Off" is one word), so put a literal `{" "}` between the parts of a button or menu item that are separate elements (flex items draw no whitespace, so the look does not change).
- State is never conveyed by colour alone; give it text in the accessible name and a shape or glyph on screen.
- Landmarks and headings stay meaningful (`header`, `main`, labelled `section`s, one `h1`).
- Dialogs go through `Modal`; a destructive confirmation starts focus on the safe choice.

## CSS

- Shared styles live in `apps/web/src/styles/`, all imported by `styles/index.css`, which `main.tsx` alone imports. A game's own sheet (`games/sm/sm.css`, `games/smz3/smz3.css`) is imported by its `module.ts`, so it loads with the game, and so is `games/board.css`, what those two boards share (the panels of toggles `board-panel` and `toggle-chip`, the run type dialog's `mode-*` fields, `stream-url`, the grid cells' `board-grid__cells` and `board-cell__count`; the stream view's placement of `board-grid` is in `styles/obs.css`); the application menu's (`desktop/appMenu.css`) by `desktop/AppMenuItem.tsx`. `reset.css` stays first in that list.
- Class names are written literally in the JSX; there is no `CSS_CLASSES` map.
- CSS Grid for layout, never tables; a `<table>` is for tabular data only (the shortcuts list). Empty grid cells render a spacer rather than `null`.
- Colours, spacing and sizes come from the tokens in `variables.css`; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Verify in the browser

After any visible change, run `pnpm test:e2e` (overflow, overlapping regions, console errors and failed requests, axe, keyboard reachability at 390 / 820 / 1440 / 1920 px), and look at the page yourself: screenshots of each width via Playwright, since a test passing is not the same as the design being right. Extend `apps/web/e2e/tracker.e2e.ts` when a new behaviour needs a real browser.
