# UI, styles and accessibility

## Components (`apps/web/src/components/`)

- `tracker/` — `TrackerGrid` → `grid/GridItem` routes a cell: empty → spacer, `keysN` → `BigKeyCell`, the key cell of key dungeon N (`KEY_DUNGEONS` in `logic/settings.ts`: the grid's ten, then Hyrule Castle, Castle Tower and Ganon's Tower on the 8th row) (`KeyToggle` buttons for what its pool holds, `ownItems`: the big key and the small keys, none for Eastern Palace, whose pool has no small key, no big key for Hyrule Castle or Castle Tower; in runs whose maps and compasses leave their dungeons (MC, MCS and Full Shuffle: `wildItems` in `data/presets.ts`) only, the map and compass on top, the small keys' count then a badge on the key), `keylabelN` → `KeyLabel` (a castle dungeon's short name, HC, CT, GT, as keysanity trackers label their key rows; decorative), in a retro run no small key (generic keys are no dungeon's) and, on the 8th row, the shops' cells (`itemGridFor`), the key cells' rows showing only with the key cells (`visibleRows`; `layout--key-rows` sizes `--cell` for 8 rows), `boss*` → `BossCell` (a `<fieldset>` named after the dungeon: the boss button plus `MedallionPicker`, `ChestCounter`, `RewardPicker` as sibling buttons from `overlays/BossOverlays.tsx`; Ganon's Tower's, `boss12` at the end of the 8th row, which therefore always shows, holds Agahnim 2 (`agahnim2`) and its items left (`chest12`, up to 27: the `chestN.png` counters run to 27) and no reward), else → `Item`.
- Every grid control spreads `useGridFocusable({ row, col, slot })` (roving tab index, arrow-key position; slot 0 is the cell's main control, then reading order) and `useStepHandlers(step, useControlShortcuts(control).aria)` (click forward, right-click or "-" back, touch long-press ignored; `aria-keyshortcuts` lists the control's keyboard shortcuts and "-"). Never put a button inside another.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `hooks/useItemShortcuts.ts` is the one window listener; a bound key steps its control through `stepControl`, exactly as a click does. It shares `isAwayFromBoard` (`apps/web/src/utils/keyboard.ts`: typing in a field, a dialog open) with `useUndoShortcuts`, skips IME composition and auto-repeat, and never takes a Ctrl/Cmd chord or a key that keeps its own job (`refusalOf` in `data/keymap.ts`). The focused cell's "-" gives way to a shortcut on the same physical key.
- `map/MapTracker.tsx` draws two square worlds (`map-world-light` / `-dark`), side by side, stacked or `auto` (stacks below 35rem via a container query). Coordinates are authored against the whole asset and placed in their world by `toWorldPosition` (`apps/web/src/utils`): the alltpr pack's markers are in that space (percent of the whole image). It draws the run's world's (`markersOf`): an Inverted run moves Link's House to the Dark World, puts the Bomb Merchant in its place and swaps Castle Tower's and Ganon's Tower's doors.
- `map/MapMarkers.tsx` — `MapLocation` (one location or a cave's several: a click checks them all, or none once all are) and `MapDungeon` (a decorative, `aria-hidden` square showing the counter by the pigeonhole rule, under the boss button, which toggles the boss's grid flag) are `<button>`s named by the marker's name plus their state, with a glyph per state besides the colour: ✓ available, ? possible, an eye (a CSS mask, since emoji fonts colour the character) for seen — out of logic but in sight, from the engine's visible channel —, ✕ out of logic. The precedence is `locationStatus`'s: available > possible > seen > unavailable. They read their state through the hooks of `apps/web/src/logic/solution.ts` (`useMarkerStatus`, `useBossStatus`), which return strings, so a click re-renders only the markers it changed. Markers keep a 24 px floor; where real locations sit closer than that, WCAG 2.5.8's map-pin exception applies and the e2e target-size check skips `.mapspan`.
- `map/MapPanel.tsx` — the map panel: ARIA tabs Map | Checks | Run, with Entrances after Checks while entrances are shuffled (`ui/Tabs.tsx`) over one tabpanel, `#map-view`, which shows the worlds and their legend, `ChecksPanel` or `RunPanel`, by the `mapView` preference; `VoiceControl`, `RunTimer` and `GoMode` sit beside the tabs (`map-panel__status`). Over the map and the checks, `LogicNote` says why every check is maybe while the run's logic is not modelled (`logicModelled`), and in a `crossedLogic` run that the logic follows the entrances noted. "Skip to map" and "Skip to checks" render their view with `flushSync`, then focus `#map-view`.
- `map/entrances/` — the entrance assistant, for a run with shuffled entrances, each module loaded on demand with the pack's entrance tables (`logic/entrances.ts`): `EntranceLayer` (a shuffled world's markers, each check inside a place the shuffle mixes standing above the first door noted to lead there or nowhere yet (`placeMarkers`; the Dam's, half outdoors, stays), then its shuffled doors and holes as round `mapspan` buttons; pointing at a noted one rings every entrance into the same place (`entrancePickerStore.linked`); an empty dashed ring while not known, filled by the kind of place once noted with a glyph for each, named "Kakariko Shop: leads to Elder House (East)", with a `text` tooltip), `EntrancePicker` (the `Modal` "Where does … lead?": a search, then the targets the shuffle may give it by kind as buttons (`targetsOf`), a hole and its cave's door noted together as one step (`notesFor`, `noteEntrances`); one noted elsewhere says where, and choosing it moves it; "Not known" forgets one) and `EntrancesPanel` (the Entrances tab: every entrance by world, its destination beside it, a search, how many are noted and that autotracking notes each door gone through outside Race Mode). The markers and the list open the picker through `entrancePickerStore`.
- `map/ChecksPanel.tsx` — every location of the map's markers as a list, by zone (`logic/checks.ts`: a dungeon's under its name, any other under its upstream region's). Status filters with their counts (available and possible shown at first), a zone `<select>` and a search narrow it, and a `role="status"` summary says how many rows show. A row is a checkbox calling `toggleChecks([id])`, its state in words beside the map's glyph (`check-swatch state-glyph`), and for a state other than available or done a "Why?" disclosure with `MissingLines`, mounted only once opened, and only while the run's logic is applied (`solutionOf(…).modelled`: not while a crossed run's logic loads). A dungeon whose counter is 0 shows its rows checked and disabled. The panel subscribes to every state at once (`checkStates` through `useShallow`) and rows are memoized; a row a filter hides once ticked hands its focus to the next row. Zone headings are sticky and take the page's fixed background (`.page-background`). Beside the grid it scrolls inside the viewport's height; under it, it grows with the page.
- `map/RunPanel.tsx` — the Run tab: the run at a glance (`<dl>`: the timer's time, ticking only while it runs; the checks done, `checkStates`; the bosses beaten), the splits (a `<table>`: the bosses in the order they fell, from `splitsOf` in `logic/runLog.ts`, each with its time and the segment since the last, then the goal once the timer stopped there), the timeline (an `<ol>`, newest first: each find's `<time>`, or a dash said as "before the timer", its sprite as the board draws it, `findIcon`, and its name, `findName`; rows memoized) and "From the game", the ROM's own stats (`reading.progress.romStats`, in the end credits' order) outside races once a file is loaded, or why not. Beside the grid each column scrolls inside the viewport's height; under it, the panel grows with the page. It reads the timeline (`logic/timeline.ts`) with one selector.
- `map/GoMode.tsx` — the run's go mode (`goModeStatusOf`): ☆ not yet, ★? possible, ★ in logic, with its words; hidden for a goal without one, and a plain label "Go mode: goal unknown" while a Mystery run's goal is hidden. It is a button opening a popover (the Popover API, placed under it on click) with what the go mode is missing (`useGoModeMissing`, `WaysList`); a visually hidden `role="status"` announces a change.
- `map/RunTimer.tsx` — the run timer (`timer` in the store, `logic/timer.ts`): a button showing the time (tabular figures) and the state's glyph (▷ not started, ▶ running, ❚❚ paused, ✓ stopped at the goal) with its words in the accessible name, opening a popover like `GoMode`'s (`placeUnder` in `apps/web/src/utils/popover.ts`): the time as `role="timer"`, Start / Pause / Resume, Reset, the `autoTimer` checkbox ("Start and stop with the game") and, outside Race Mode while the game is in play, the ROM's own play time (`romStats.playFrames` → `playTimeMs`). Only it re-renders each second, and only while running (`hooks/useNow.ts`, ticking on the second's boundary); a visually hidden `role="status"` says a change of state, never the ticks.
- `map/VoiceControl.tsx` — voice commands: `VoiceControl`, a button beside the run timer whose words and microphone icon say the status (in sight for as long as the tracker listens; a command heard shows on it for 3 s, "…: no change" when it changed nothing), opening a `Modal`: what voice commands do and that no sound leaves the computer, the status with what to do on a failure, the microphone (`enumerateDevices`, listed again once listening has named them), Start / Stop listening, and "What to say" (`VOICE_GUIDE` of `logic/voice.ts`, phrases in `<q>`). `VoiceLiveRegion` says each command heard. The engine (`voice/engine.ts`: Vosk in a Web Worker, an AudioWorklet handing the microphone's samples on, the model kept in IndexedDB) is a chunk of its own, imported when listening starts; `voice/apply.ts` does what a command says through the store (`setItem`, `setSmallKeys`, `handleMedallionChange`, undo, redo, the timer), leaving a control the board hides alone (`isControlShown`, shared with the shortcuts).
- `ui/Missing.tsx` — the words of what a check is missing: `MissingLines` ("Missing", "In logic with", "Possible with", "Maybe" lines), `WaysList` (each thing with its icon; "a door you have not noted" for `Ways.door`, what no item brings in a crossed run), `doubtText`. The tooltip and the Checks list's "Why?" share them.
- `ui/Tooltip.tsx` — the page's single tooltip, driven by `stores/tooltipStore.ts` (kept out of the game store so hovering never re-renders the board). Controls opt in with `useTooltip({ kind, … })`: mouse hover, keyboard focus, or a touch long-press (a tap stays the control's action). A map marker's (`kind: "marker"`, or a grid boss cell's `kind: "dungeon"`) lists each location it stands for with its state, and what one not in logic is missing (`MissingLines`, read from the run's analysis, never worked out on hover); a dungeon's says the items its counter has left and how many are surely in logic. The marker's accessible name says its state, so the tooltip is `aria-hidden`. When several hooks give one element the same handler, combine them with `mergeHandlers` (`apps/web/src/utils/mergeHandlers.ts`), never by spreading twice.
- `ui/` — `Header`, `Tabs` (the ARIA tabs pattern, automatic activation; the setup guide's systems and the map panel's views), `Modal` (native `<dialog>` + `showModal()`; `wide` scrolls a long body between a fixed header and footer), `ConfirmDialog` (opens on Cancel via `data-autofocus`), `PresetSelector` (the run types; the summary's tunable settings, `TUNING` in `data/presets.ts` (goal, dungeon items, swords, crystals, item placement, pool, functionality, accessibility), are labelled selects that change the run in place through `updateSettings`; in a Mystery run, or while hidden, a hideable one offers "? Unknown" (`hideSetting`); and `SeedImport`: a seed's alttpr.com link or hash, its settings checked against the board's run type with `checkSeed`; another run type goes to the same confirmation as a preset, then `applyRun`), `TrackerControls`, `BigKeyToggle`, `ShortcutsButton` → `ShortcutsDialog` (rebinding, conflicts, reset, the master switch), `KeyChord` (a chord as `<kbd>` keys, labelled for the player's layout), `StreamViewControl` (the `?view=obs` dialog; its checkbox adds `&timer=1`), `StreamTimer` (the stream view's run timer: an SVG one cell high in the chest counters' 3×5 font and frame, `pixelText`, every width even so it centres on a whole pixel; `ObsView` keeps it a row under the grid), `ToolbarMenu` (the More menu button, ARIA menu button pattern), `UpdatePrompt` (the non-modal "new version" notice; never reload on its own).
- `autotrack/` — `AutotrackControl` (the toolbar's plug with its status dot and race-legal badge; `variant="menu"` for More), `AutotrackLiveRegion` (the polite `role="status"`, outside the foldable control, quiet while merely connecting; it adds autotracking's `news`, a switch to the seed's run type, after the status), `SeedPrompt` (the notice after `UpdatePrompt` asking to switch to the seed's run type, Switch or Keep mine, while a board with a run on it differs from the seed's), `AutotrackMoreBadge` (the dot on More once the control folds), and the lazy `AutotrackDialog` (status, the seed's run type (`autotrack-seed`, `seedLineText`), Connect/Disconnect/Retry now, Stop waiting while another tab autotracks, diagnostic report, Sync from game with what it changed and, in Race Mode, what stays the player's to mark (where entrances lead too, in a run that shuffles them), `SetupGuide` with `Tabs` per OS and the steps ticked from `setupProgress`, `Readout` "What I read" with the seed's settings, titled "from alttpr.com" when a race seed's came from its file, "from the ROM and alttpr.com" when the file adds what no ROM states, and what the board has no cell for). The dialog opens on a click only: autotracking never pops anything up during a run. Its words live in `apps/web/src/autotrack/` (`status.ts` headlines and dot phases, `guide.ts` fixes, `readout.ts` names). No cell or marker says it was set by the game: once connected the game sets nearly all of them, so a mark would say little and cost every name a few words.
- The toolbar folds by measured fit, never by breakpoint: system fonts differ between macOS, Windows and Linux. `hooks/useToolbarFit.ts` lays a hidden copy of the bar out in each form (`FITS`, richest first) and writes the richest that holds — the full bar on one row, a folded one on two — to its `data-fit`, and what it folds to `data-fold` tokens, which `ui.css` and `styles/toolbar-menu.css` follow. The fold order: labels, then the secondary actions (Export, Import, Stream view, Shortcuts) into More, then the map layout (`menuitemradio`s in a group), then the key cells toggle (a `menuitemcheckbox`), then New game to an icon, and last Autotracking into More (the first item there, its dot copied onto the More button); Mode and Undo/Redo never fold. More reads `data-fold` as it opens to know which settings it holds. The hook re-checks on resize, content change and font load, moves up only with 24 px to spare, and never re-renders React. Never key toolbar CSS on a viewport width. `StreamViewControl` and `ShortcutsButton` take `variant="menu"` to render as a menu item with their own dialog. React bubbles a portalled dialog's events through its React parents, so a menu item's dialog still reaches the menu's handlers: they act only on events from the menu's own DOM.
- Subscribe to the store with one selector per slice, never bare `useGameStore()`.

## Semantics and accessibility

- Every interactive element is a semantic `<button>` (or link) with an accessible name; never a clickable `div`, and never a button inside a button. A native button fires click on Enter and Space, so add no key handlers for them.
- State is never conveyed by colour alone; give it text in the accessible name and a shape or glyph on screen.
- Landmarks and headings stay meaningful (`header`, `main`, labelled `section`s, one `h1`).
- Dialogs go through `Modal`; a destructive confirmation starts focus on the safe choice.

## CSS

- Styles live in `apps/web/src/styles/`, all imported by `styles/index.css`, which `main.tsx` alone imports. `reset.css` stays first in that list.
- Class names are written literally in the JSX; there is no `CSS_CLASSES` map.
- CSS Grid for layout, never tables; a `<table>` is for tabular data only (the shortcuts list). Empty grid cells render a spacer rather than `null`.
- Colours, spacing and sizes come from the tokens in `variables.css`; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Verify in the browser

After any visible change, run `pnpm test:e2e` (overflow, overlapping regions, console errors and failed requests, axe, keyboard reachability at 390 / 820 / 1440 / 1920 px), and look at the page yourself: screenshots of each width via Playwright, since a test passing is not the same as the design being right. Extend `apps/web/e2e/tracker.e2e.ts` when a new behaviour needs a real browser.
